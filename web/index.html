      Comparison

      Checked against NASA

      • Sun and Earth positions match JPL Horizons to better than 0.0001° (Malapert, December 2026).
      • GPU horizons match an independent float64 calculation to within 0.0024°.
      • Ground heights at Apollo, Chang'e-4 and Chang'e-6 landers match LROC's published values within 7 m.

      Data

      NASA LRO LOLA elevation (LDEM_80S_20M, LDEM_64); NAIF SPICE DE421 and lunar orientation kernels; JPL Horizons; USGS Artemis III candidate regions (McClernan 2024, doi:10.5066/P1MEQ6UK). Site coordinates from LROC, NASA NSSDC and ISRO.

      How it was made

      Horizons for every 20 m pixel were traced with custom Metal GPU kernels on an Apple M5 Max (360 directions, 200 km, lunar curvature). Code written with AI assistance (Claude, Anthropic) and reviewed by the author. Not an official NASA product.
